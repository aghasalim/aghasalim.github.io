Projects

latent-diffusion-from-scratch

Autoencoder, DDPM and DDIM. Diffusion in a 4x compressed latent trains 5.3x faster than the pixel baseline and scores 6.2x better at matched steps.

rlhf-ppo-from-scratch

PPO, a Bradley-Terry reward model, plus DPO, GRPO, RLOO and Best-of-N. The proxy reward climbs to +9.97 while the true objective peaks and then collapses to -1.43, below the policy it started from.

rectified-flow-from-scratch

Conditional flow matching and reflow. One round of reflow drops the straightness metric by 15000x and buys single-step sampling at 128x less compute for no measurable loss.

flash-attention-from-scratch

IO-aware attention, measured rather than assumed. Kernel fusion is worth about 3x and lifts achieved throughput from 22% of peak to roughly 67%.

schrodinger-bridge-from-scratch

Entropic optimal transport, diffusion Schrodinger bridges by IPF, and bridge matching. Bridge matching wins by 1.3x to 12x at a quarter the cost.

mla-from-scratch

Multi-head latent attention from DeepSeek-V2, with decoupled RoPE and absorbed inference verified equal to the naive form to 6e-07. A 14.2x KV cache reduction, 120 GB down to 8.4 GB at 128k context.

world-model-from-scratch

An RSSM and an actor critic trained entirely in imagination, on a partially observable task where velocity is hidden. The world model works and the agent that learns inside it does not, which the write-up says plainly.

vla-from-scratch

Four ways a robot policy can emit a continuous action, on demonstrations that route around an obstacle either way. A regression head learns the average of the two and spends 8.44 of 24 steps pressed into the wall, against 3.56 for a flow matching head running at 90x the control rate of a diffusion one.

spurious-ad

A planted-confound benchmark for unsupervised anomaly detection that refutes its own premise. AUROC rises to 0.998 while heatmap peaks on the real defect fall from 0.75 to 0.37, a 4.2x effect. Pinning the training confound rate removes it entirely, so it was never a label shortcut. Replicated on real MVTec images across two detectors and two backbones.

vlm-hallucination-eval

Object hallucination measured against a hand-verified adversarial set. Accuracy barely moves with phrasing but the hallucination rate more than doubles, 6.0% to 13.4%, when the object is smuggled into the premise. A CLIP verifier cuts it by 25 to 40% relative and costs recall. Live demo.

lora-forgetting

LoRA on Qwen2.5-1.5B for expense extraction, trained on a laptop. Every-field exact match rises 46.7% to 75.6%, ARC moves 0.7 points, and the per-slice breakdown shows two slices getting worse under the headline. Two ARC scoring protocols disagree by 16.7 points on the same model.

rl-arm-reward-shaping

A 2-link arm, six reward functions, two exploited outright. The distance reward taught the agent to crash on purpose in 200 of 200 episodes. Final PPO agent 43.2% success against 73.5% for a PD controller, but 17.3% collisions against 25.5%. Rewards replayed in seven languages by CI. Live demo.

enas-microcontroller

Evolutionary architecture search under a hard 50k parameter, 250 KB int8 budget for Cortex-M devices. 3.7 accuracy points over the hand baseline at equal footprint, with the search's own defects, selecting on test and one seed, written up before the gain.

groundedness

Claim-level hallucination check for RAG answers in any language, pip install groundedness. Includes my own XLM-RoBERTa detector trained on 30 languages, served from a Raspberry Pi 5 in Baku and published on Hugging Face, with the paper on SSRN. On 434 cases across 31 languages it catches 352 of 372 planted errors, against 265 for Vectara HHEM. Live.

siba-meristem-1.0

Qwen2.5-3B fine-tuned with QLoRA on 167,785 instruction pairs in Azerbaijani, Russian, English and Turkish. Shipped as a 1.9 GB GGUF, so it runs locally with ollama run aghasalim/siba-meristem-1.0. On Hugging Face and Ollama.

Model 3B parameters

azbench

An Azerbaijani LLM benchmark: Belebele, INCLUDE and TUMLU exam questions, 2148 items, zero-shot, scored by exact letter against any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

groundcheck-mcp

An MCP connector that checks whether a claim is actually grounded, a real quote, a real citation, real code output, with no second language model doing the judging. Works with Claude and Gemini.

eu-ai-act-rag

Retrieval over Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, graded by a model that is not the one answering. 90.9% hit rate at 6 and 90.2% faithful on 45 hand-written questions.

ieee-fraud-ml

IEEE-CIS fraud detection done the honest way rather than the version that leaks future data. 0.1044 AUC is what that difference costs, and 0.9086 on the private leaderboard, scored against labels I never saw.
